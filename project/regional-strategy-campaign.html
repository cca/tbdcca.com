SPUR is a member-supported nonprofit organization that brings people together from across the political spectrum to develop solutions to the big problems our cities face. With offices in San Francisco, San Jose and Oakland, SPUR is recognized as a leading civic planning organization and is respected for their independent and holistic approach to urban issues.

SPUR approached TBD* with the need to create propoganda posters that visualize and stand against some of the important issues in the Bay Area Region. 

“Ryan and Batul were such good listeners and the design solutions they came up with were absolutely perfect for our needs. It wasn't the easiest problem to conceptualize but they not only nailed it, they became urbanists in the process. Hearing that Batul had shifted from UBER to bikeshare while researching this project just made our hearts swell. But more importantly, the posters are GORGEOUS and we're so excited to share them widely.” —Allison Arieff, SPUR
