NIAD is a community based art studio and gallery for adult artists with disabilities. Through creative expression NIAD promotes independence, dignity, and creates space in the contemporary art world that is accessible and inclusive for everyone.

NIAD artists make expressive, abstract, and colorful contemporary art in many forms. We want to express the free creative flow, joy, and independence of NIAD in Win Win 12. We want to express the personal aspect as well as creative energy in this campaign.

Win Win fundraising event is on May 18th, 2024. The theme is “I Make Art in Richmond” NIAD has a uniquely Richmond spirit.  NIAD wants to raise $95k to further fund their artists. With so many fundraisers and art events happening in the spring, we want to help NIAD stand out as something unique.
